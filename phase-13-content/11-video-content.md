# Video Content

---

## What It Is

Product tutorials, feature update videos, and podcast guest appearances. This covers the production pipeline for each type.

## Product Tutorials

Short videos showing how to accomplish a specific task in your product.

### Structure

| Section | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 5 seconds | "Here's how to [achieve outcome] in [Product]" |
| Context | 10 seconds | When you'd use this feature, who it's for |
| Walkthrough | 45 seconds | Screen recording of the steps, with text overlays |
| Result | 10 seconds | Show the completed outcome |
| CTA | 5 seconds | "Try it yourself — link in description" |

Total: 75 seconds per tutorial.

### Production Rules

**Design decisions:**
- Record with Screen Studio — it adds smooth cursor animations, zoom effects, and transitions automatically
- No voiceover — use text overlays and captions. Keeps production fast and works for all audiences (different languages, sound-off viewing).
- Background music: subtle, upbeat, royalty-free. Same track across all tutorials for brand consistency.
- One tutorial per feature. Don't combine 3 features into one video — users search for specific tasks.
- Title format: "How to [do thing] in [Product]" — matches search intent
- Host on YouTube — free, embeddable, SEO value, auto-generates transcripts
- Embed on the relevant feature page and in the knowledge base article for that feature

### Production Pipeline

1. Identify the feature to cover (from the most-asked support questions or new feature launches)
2. Write the script: 3 sentences of context + step-by-step walkthrough
3. Set up the product with clean, realistic data (no "test123")
4. Record with Screen Studio in one take. If you make a mistake, re-record — don't try to edit mid-recording.
5. Add text overlays for each step
6. Export and upload to YouTube with title, description (include the target keyword), and tags
7. Embed on the feature page and knowledge base
8. Create a tweet with the video (native upload, not a YouTube link — native video gets more reach)

## Feature Update Videos

Short videos announcing a new feature or improvement.

### Structure

| Section | Duration | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Before | 10 seconds | Show the problem or old way of doing it |
| After | 15 seconds | Show the new feature solving it |
| Details | 15 seconds | Quick walkthrough of how it works |
| CTA | 5 seconds | "Available now — try it" |

Total: 45 seconds per update video.

### Production Rules

**Design decisions:**
- Same recording setup as tutorials (Screen Studio, no voiceover, text overlays)
- Before/after format is the most engaging structure for update videos
- Post natively on Twitter/X (not as a YouTube link) — native video gets the strongest algorithmic boost
- Also post on LinkedIn for B2B audiences
- Include in the changelog entry (phase-13-content/08-changelog.md) as the visual

## Podcast Guest Appearances

Getting invited onto podcasts in your niche to reach new audiences.

### How to Get Booked

**Design decisions:**
- Identify 20 podcasts in your niche — search "[your category] podcast" on Apple Podcasts and Spotify
- Filter for podcasts with 50 episodes or fewer
- Send a pitch email: who you are (1 sentence), what you built and why (2 sentences), 3 specific topics you can discuss, a link to your product
- Personalise every pitch — reference a specific episode you listened to
- Follow up once after 7 days if no response. Don't follow up more than once.
- Batch outreach: pitch 5 podcasts per week until booked

### Preparation

**Design decisions:**
- Prepare 3 talking points with specific stories and numbers — not generic advice
- Have your elevator pitch ready (from the copy bank — phase-13-content/01-foundational-copy-bank.md)
- Mention your product naturally during the conversation — once when introducing yourself and once when a topic connects to what you built. Not more.
- Ask the host to include a link to your product in the show notes
- After the episode airs: share on social media, tag the host, add to your "As seen on" section if you have one

## Publishing Cadence

**Design decisions:**
- Product tutorials: 1 per month (or 1 per new feature, whichever comes first)
- Feature update videos: 1 per major feature launch
- Podcast outreach: 5 pitches per week until you have 2 bookings per month, then maintain 2 per month

## What to Test

- Tutorial videos are under 75 seconds
- Feature update videos are under 45 seconds
- Videos play correctly on YouTube and when embedded on feature pages
- Native video uploads on Twitter/X play correctly
- Text overlays are readable at mobile size
- Product data in recordings is clean and realistic
- YouTube title includes the target keyword
- Knowledge base articles embed the relevant tutorial video

## Edge Cases

- **Feature changes after tutorial is published** — re-record the affected steps. Don't leave outdated tutorials live — they cause support tickets when users follow instructions that no longer match the UI.
- **Podcast host asks about competitors** — be respectful and factual. Acknowledge what competitors do well, explain how your approach is different. Don't trash competitors on a recorded podcast.
- **Video gets more views than expected** — pin a comment with a link to sign up. Update the video description with a clear CTA if it's missing.
- **YouTube removes the video for policy violation** — usually caused by music licensing. Use only royalty-free music with a verified license.

## Implementation Checklist

- [ ] Screen Studio configured for recording
- [ ] Recording template set up (clean product data, consistent window size)
- [ ] Royalty-free background music track selected (same track for all videos)
- [ ] YouTube channel created and branded (from phase-14 Go-To-Market)
- [ ] First product tutorial recorded and published
- [ ] Tutorial embedded on relevant feature page and knowledge base article
- [ ] Podcast pitch email template created
- [ ] 20 target podcasts identified
- [ ] Publishing cadence: 1 tutorial per month, feature update videos on major launches, 5 podcast pitches per week

## Common Mistakes

- Videos over 2 minutes (attention drops after 60 seconds for tutorials)
- Voiceover on early videos (slows production, text overlays are faster and multilingual)
- YouTube link on Twitter instead of native video upload (reduced reach)
- Product data showing "test123" or Lorem ipsum in recordings
- Recording the walkthrough with a cluttered, unrealistic product state
- Not embedding tutorials on the relevant feature page (video lives only on YouTube)
- Generic podcast pitches (not personalised to the host)
- Mentioning your product 10 times on a podcast (once at intro, once when relevant)
