# Tweets / X Posts

---

## What It Is

Tweets and threads on X that drive engagement, build audience, and convert followers into users.

## Post Types

| Type | Purpose | Format |
|---|---|---|
| Single tweet | Quick take, product update, stat | 1 tweet, under 280 characters |
| Thread | Deep insight, tutorial, story | 7 tweets (hook + 5 value tweets + CTA) |
| Product update | New feature, improvement | Single tweet with screenshot or GIF |
| Engagement bait | Start a conversation | Question or controversial take |

## Thread Structure (7 Tweets)

**Tweet 1 — Hook:**
- Bold statement, surprising stat, or contrarian take
- Include specific numbers ("I gained 500 users in 30 days" not "I grew my user base quickly")
- Create tension — the reader must need to know what comes next
- No links in the hook tweet (algorithm deprioritises link tweets)

**Tweets 2-6 — Value:**
- One key point per tweet
- Each tweet is self-contained — it makes sense even if someone sees it without the thread context
- Use line breaks for readability (not a wall of text)
- Include a visual every 3 tweets (screenshot, chart, meme from memethropic.com API)

**Tweet 7 — CTA:**
- Summarise the thread's takeaway in one sentence
- CTA: "If you want to [outcome], try [product]" with a link to the landing page
- Ask for engagement: "Follow for more [topic]" or "Repost if this was useful"
- The link goes in the last tweet only, never in the hook

## Single Tweet Formulas

**The Hot Take:**
"Most [audience] think [common belief]. But [contrarian truth]. Here's why: [one sentence reason]."

**The Stat Drop:**
"[Specific number] of [audience] [surprising fact]. [One sentence takeaway]. [Link or CTA]."

**The Before/After:**
"Before [product/approach]: [pain]. After: [outcome]. [Screenshot or GIF]."

**The Product Update:**
"Just shipped: [feature name]. [One sentence what it does]. [Screenshot or GIF]. Try it: [link]."

**The Question:**
"What's your biggest challenge with [topic]? (Building something to solve this)"

## Content Rules

**Design decisions:**
- Tweet about what your customers achieve, not what you sell. "How this founder cut their invoice time by 80%" not "Our invoice tool has automated reminders."
- 80/20 rule: 80% value content (insights, tips, stories), 20% product content (updates, CTAs).
- Post 1 tweet per day minimum.
- Schedule via Postiz — batch-create a week of tweets on Monday, schedule for the week.
- Threads perform best posted at 9am in your audience's primary timezone.
- Never post a bare link with no context. Always add commentary, a take, or a question above the link.

## Algorithm Rules (2026)

**Design decisions:**
- Keep value content on-platform — put the insight in the tweet, not behind a link.
- Write tweets that invite replies and quote tweets.
- When linking to a blog post, put the link in the last tweet of a thread or in a reply to the main tweet, not in the hook.
- Include screen recordings of product features as short video tweets.

## Production Pipeline

1. Check keyword research (phase-13-content/03-keyword-research.md) and blog post pipeline for topics
2. Claude drafts the tweet or thread from a topic + format
3. Review: does it have a hook? Does it provide value? Does it sound human?
4. Add visual if applicable (screenshot, GIF, meme)
5. Schedule via Postiz
6. After posting: engage with replies for the first hour (algorithm boosts tweets with early engagement)

## What to Test

- Threads are 7 tweets with hook, value, and CTA structure
- No links in hook tweets
- Visuals appear every 3 tweets in threads
- Product mentions stay at 20% of total posts
- Posting cadence is 1 per day minimum
- Scheduled posts go out at the correct time via Postiz

## Edge Cases

- **Thread gets high engagement but tweet 7 (CTA) doesn't** — the CTA is too salesy or disconnected from the thread topic. Rewrite the CTA to connect directly to the thread's value.
- **Engagement drops after switching to more product content** — exceeded the 80/20 ratio. Pull back to value content and rebuild engagement before promoting again.
- **Someone quote-tweets with criticism** — engage respectfully. Don't delete or block unless it's harassment. Constructive criticism in a quote tweet often drives more visibility to your original post.
- **Algorithm changes reduce reach** — review posting format quarterly and adjust based on current performance data.

## Common Mistakes

- Links in the hook tweet
- Wall of text with no line breaks
- More than 20% product content
- Threads longer than 10 tweets
- No visual breaks in threads
- Posting inconsistently
- Not engaging with replies
- Generic corporate voice instead of personal founder voice
- Tweeting about features instead of outcomes
