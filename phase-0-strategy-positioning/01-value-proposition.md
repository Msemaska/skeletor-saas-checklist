# Value Proposition / One-Liner

---

## What It Is

A single sentence that explains what your product does, who it's for, and why it matters. This is the foundation every other piece of copy builds on.

## Formula

**[Product] helps [audience] [achieve outcome] by [mechanism], without [pain point].**

### Examples
- "Calendly helps busy professionals schedule meetings by sharing a booking link, without the back-and-forth emails."
- "Linear helps engineering teams track work by combining speed with simplicity, without the bloat of Jira."
- "Plausible helps website owners understand their traffic by providing simple analytics, without compromising visitor privacy."

## Process

### Step 1: Answer These Questions
- What does your product do in plain language?
- Who specifically is this for? (role, not demographics)
- What outcome does the user get?
- What's the current alternative? (competitor or manual process)
- Why is your way better?
- What pain does it eliminate?

### Step 2: Draft Variants
Write 5-10 one-liners using different angles:

- **Outcome-first:** "Get [result] without [pain]"
- **Audience-first:** "For [who] who need [what]"
- **Contrast:** "Unlike [alternative], [product] does [difference]"
- **Speed/simplicity:** "[Do thing] in [timeframe]"

### Step 3: Pressure Test
- Can someone with zero context understand it in under 5 seconds?
- Does it pass the "so what?" test? (is the outcome meaningful?)
- Is it specific enough to exclude people who aren't your audience?
- Could a competitor copy-paste it and have it still make sense? (if yes, it's too generic)

### Step 4: Expand Into Supporting Copy
From the one-liner, derive:

- **Headline** (marketing site hero) — the one-liner or a punchier version
- **Subheadline** — 1-2 sentences expanding on the mechanism
- **Elevator pitch** (30 seconds) — one-liner + 2-3 supporting points
- **Boilerplate** (for press, directories) — 2-3 sentences, factual tone

## Where It Gets Used

| Location | Format |
|---|---|
| Marketing site hero | Headline + subheadline |
| Meta description | ~155 characters |
| Social media bios | Adapted per platform character limits |
| Product Hunt tagline | Max 60 characters |
| Email signature | One-liner |
| Investor/partner intros | Elevator pitch |
| App store descriptions | Expanded version |
| OG image text | Shortest possible version |

## Deliverables

- [ ] One-liner finalized (under 20 words)
- [ ] Headline + subheadline pair
- [ ] 30-second elevator pitch
- [ ] Boilerplate paragraph (2-3 sentences)
- [ ] Adapted versions for each platform (character-limited)

## Common Mistakes

- Too vague ("We help businesses grow")
- Too technical ("AI-powered NLP-driven workflow automation platform")
- Feature-focused instead of outcome-focused ("We have dashboards and reports" vs "See what's working in 30 seconds")
- Trying to appeal to everyone (if your audience is "everyone", it's no one)
