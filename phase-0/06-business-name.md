# Business Name

---

## What It Is

The name your product goes by. Not just a label — it affects discoverability, memorability, domain availability, and brand identity.

## Types of Names

| Type | Example | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| Descriptive | Salesforce, Bookkeeper | Instantly understood | Hard to trademark, generic |
| Invented | Spotify, Vercel | Unique, ownable, easy to trademark | Meaningless until you build the brand |
| Compound | YouTube, Snapchat | Memorable, hints at function | Good ones are mostly taken |
| Metaphor | Amazon, Slack | Evocative, memorable | Can feel forced if the metaphor is weak |
| Abbreviation | IBM, AWS | Short, professional | No personality, hard to discover early on |
| Personal | Basecamp (37signals) | Authentic | Harder to sell/transfer |

**For SaaS:** invented and compound names tend to work best. Descriptive names are hard to own and rank for.

## Process

### Step 1: Generate Candidates

Brainstorm 20-30 names across different approaches:

- **Word mashups:** combine two relevant words (Drop + box = Dropbox)
- **Truncation:** shorten a longer word (Kubernetes → K8s, Figma from "figment")
- **Latin/Greek roots:** use root words for relevant concepts
- **Action words:** what does your product help people *do*?
- **Metaphors:** what does using your product *feel like*?
- **AI-assisted:** ask Claude to generate 50 name candidates with constraints

### Step 2: Filter

For each candidate, check:

- [ ] .com or acceptable TLD available?
- [ ] Social media handles available? (Twitter, GitHub, LinkedIn at minimum)
- [ ] No trademark conflicts? (USPTO, EUIPO search)
- [ ] Easy to spell after hearing it once?
- [ ] Easy to pronounce in English?
- [ ] Under 10 characters? (ideal, not required)
- [ ] No negative meanings in major languages?
- [ ] Not too similar to a well-known brand?
- [ ] Works as a verb? ("Just Slack them" — bonus, not required)

### Step 3: Test

- Say it out loud 10 times. Does it still sound good?
- Write it in lowercase, uppercase, camelCase. Does it look good?
- Tell 3 people the name and ask them to spell it back
- Google it — what comes up? Will you be competing for search with an existing term?
- Sleep on it for 48 hours

### Step 4: Finalize

Once chosen:
- Purchase domain immediately (Phase 0: Domain)
- Register social media handles immediately (even if you don't use them yet)
- Check trademark feasibility (consult a lawyer if this is a serious venture)

## Deliverables

- [ ] 20-30 candidates brainstormed
- [ ] Top 5 filtered through availability checks
- [ ] Final name chosen
- [ ] Domain secured
- [ ] Social media handles registered
- [ ] Trademark conflict check done

## Common Mistakes

- Spending weeks on the name (it matters less than you think — execution matters more)
- Choosing a name that requires explanation ("it's [word] but spelled backwards with a...")
- Picking something trendy that will age poorly
- Not checking global implications (brand names that work in English but mean something else elsewhere)
- Choosing a name too close to a competitor (legal risk + confusion)
