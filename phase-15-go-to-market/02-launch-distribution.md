# Launch Distribution

---

## What It Is

Where to announce your product at launch to get initial users, backlinks, and visibility. This covers the specific platforms, how to prepare each listing, and the launch day sequence.

## Launch Platforms

### Product Hunt

**Design decisions:**
- Launch on a Tuesday
- Post at 12:01 AM PST (Product Hunt resets daily at midnight PST)
- Tagline: under 60 characters, from the copy bank (phase-14-content/01-foundational-copy-bank.md)
- Description: under 500 characters, from the copy bank's short description
- Gallery: 5 images from the visual asset library (phase-14-content/02-visual-asset-library.md) — first image is the hero, include product screenshots, feature GIFs, and the demo video thumbnail
- First comment: the maker comment, pre-written in the copy bank. Post immediately after the listing goes live.
- Hunter: launch yourself (self-hunting)
- Engage with every comment throughout the day

### Hacker News (Show HN)

**Design decisions:**
- Title format: "Show HN: [Product Name] – [one-line description]"
- Post body: 3 paragraphs. What it does, why you built it (the problem), and a link. Keep it technical and honest.
- Post at 8:00 AM EST on a weekday
- Respond to every comment
- Don't ask for upvotes
- Lead with how it works, not marketing speak

### Indie Hackers

**Design decisions:**
- Post in the "Launch" category
- Share the founder story: what problem you had, what you tried, why you built this
- Include revenue/user numbers if you have them
- Engage with comments

### Reddit

**Design decisions:**
- Identify 3 subreddits relevant to your product's category before launch
- Don't post a launch announcement. Instead, post a genuinely helpful how-to post that naturally mentions your product as one of the tools.
- Build karma in those subreddits for 2 weeks before launch by commenting helpfully on others' posts
- If the subreddit has a "Share what you're working on" thread, post there

### Dev.to / Hashnode (if developer audience)

**Design decisions:**
- Write a technical blog post about the problem you solved, with your product as the solution
- Not a product announcement — a technical article that happens to mention your product
- Cross-post from your blog with a canonical URL pointing to your site

### Startup Directories

Submit to these directories before launch (some have review periods):

| Directory | Notes |
|---|---|
| BetaList | Submit 3 weeks before launch (review period) |
| SaaSHub | Free listing |
| MicroLaunch | For micro-SaaS |
| Launching Next | Free listing |
| AlternativeTo | List as alternative to your competitors |
| G2 | Submit vendor profile |
| Capterra | Submit vendor profile |
| StackShare | If developer-facing |

**Design decisions:**
- Listing descriptions and assets come from the copy bank and visual asset library — don't write custom copy for each directory
- Submit to BetaList 3 weeks before launch to account for their review period
- G2 and Capterra profiles are also important for LLM optimization (phase-14-content/13-llm-optimization.md)

## Launch Day Sequence

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 12:01 AM PST | Product Hunt listing goes live |
| 12:05 AM PST | Post maker comment on Product Hunt |
| 8:00 AM EST | Show HN post |
| 8:30 AM EST | Indie Hackers launch post |
| 9:00 AM EST | Social media launch posts go out via Postiz (pre-scheduled) |
| 9:00 AM EST | Launch announcement email sent via Resend to waitlist |
| All day | Engage with every comment on PH, HN, IH, social media |

**Design decisions:**
- Launch day is a full workday of engagement — don't launch and walk away
- Have all assets, copy, and posts ready the day before. Launch day is for posting and engaging, not creating.
- The launch announcement email goes to everyone on the waitlist (phase-6-marketing-site/01-marketing-site-pages.md — waitlist page)

## What to Test

- Product Hunt listing has: tagline (60 chars), description (500 chars), 5 gallery images, maker comment drafted
- Show HN post is written and formatted correctly
- All directory submissions are complete (or submitted for review)
- Social media launch posts are pre-scheduled in Postiz
- Launch announcement email is drafted and recipient list is ready
- All links in all listings point to the correct landing page

## Edge Cases

- **Product Hunt listing doesn't get traction** — engagement in the first 2 hours is critical. Share the PH link on social media and ask your network to check it out and leave genuine comments (not just upvotes).
- **Show HN gets flagged or removed** — HN mods flag promotional posts. Ensure the post is genuinely informative and technical, not a marketing pitch. If flagged, email the mods politely.
- **Directory review takes longer than expected** — submit to BetaList and similar directories 3 weeks before launch, not 1 week.
- **Negative feedback on launch day** — respond constructively to every piece of feedback. "Thank you for the feedback — we're looking into this" is always the right first response.

## Implementation Checklist

- [ ] Show HN post drafted
- [ ] Indie Hackers launch post drafted
- [ ] Reddit subreddits identified, 2 weeks of helpful commenting done
- [ ] Dev.to / Hashnode technical post drafted (if developer audience)
- [ ] BetaList submitted 3 weeks before launch
- [ ] SaaSHub, MicroLaunch, Launching Next submitted
- [ ] AlternativeTo listing created (as alternative to competitors)
- [ ] G2 and Capterra vendor profiles submitted
- [ ] Launch day sequence documented with times
- [ ] Launch announcement email drafted for waitlist
- [ ] All assets and copy from copy bank and visual library — nothing needs to be created on launch day

## Common Mistakes

- Launching without preparing all assets the day before (scrambling on launch day)
- Asking for upvotes on Product Hunt or Hacker News
- Self-promoting on Reddit without building karma first
- Not engaging with comments on launch day (the most important engagement window)
- Submitting to directories on launch day instead of 3 weeks before
- Marketing-speak on Hacker News (the audience wants technical honesty)
- Only launching on one platform (distribute across all relevant platforms simultaneously)
- Launching on a Friday or weekend (lowest traffic on most platforms)
